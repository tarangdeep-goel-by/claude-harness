# /onboard — Fill Your Memory File

Walk me through an interactive onboarding to fill out `Meta/memory.md`. Ask me one section at a time, wait for my answers, then write them to the memory file.

## Instructions

Read `Meta/memory.md` first. Identify which sections are still empty (contain `[fill in]` or `[ ]` placeholders).

Then walk through each incomplete section one at a time in this order:

### Round 1 — About You
Ask:
- What's your role and how long have you been in this position?
- Who do you report to? (name, title)
- Do you have any direct reports?
- Give me a quick background — previous roles, technical depth (can you read code? SQL? work in Figma?), domain expertise
- How would you describe your working style? (communication preference, decision-making style, pet peeves, strengths)

### Round 2 — About Your Company / Product
Ask:
- What does your company do in one line?
- What are the core products/features you work on?
- Who are the target users? Any key segments?
- What stage is the company at? (funding, team size, growth phase)
- What are the key metrics the company/your team tracks?
- Any regulatory or compliance context I should know?

### Round 3 — Your Team
Ask:
- Walk me through the people you work with closely — name, role, and anything I should know about working with them
- Who are the key stakeholders outside your immediate team? What do they care about?

### Round 4 — Your Product Area
Ask:
- What's your product scope — what do you own?
- What's the current state of things? (where things stand as you join)
- What are the known challenges? (tech debt, user pain points, gaps)
- What's on the near-term roadmap?

### Round 5 — Tools & Processes
Ask:
- What tools does the team use? (ticketing, design, comms, meetings, docs, analytics)
- What's the sprint cadence? What ceremonies exist?
- How do PRDs, design reviews, and releases work?
- How often do you do stakeholder updates? OKR cycles?

### Round 6 — Communication
Ask:
- How do you like Claude to write for you? (tone, length, format)
- How do you typically write Slack messages, emails to stakeholders, PRDs?

### Round 7 — Goals
Ask:
- What does success look like in your first 30 days?
- What about 90 days?
- Any OKRs already set for this quarter?

## Rules

- Ask ONE round at a time. Wait for answers before moving to the next.
- It's okay if I say "don't know yet" or "skip" — leave that section with a `[TBD — update when known]` marker
- After each round, write my answers directly to `Meta/memory.md` using the Edit tool. Don't wait until the end.
- Keep questions conversational, not interrogative. I'm onboarding, not being interviewed.
- After all rounds, give me a summary of what's filled vs what's still TBD.
